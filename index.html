Welcome to Your Dashboard

Your Trust Dashboard helps you:

  • Monitor your overall data protection
  • Manage permissions with granular control

This guide will walk you through each feature. Press Next to continue →

📊

1. Trust Score Overview

The Trust Score is your primary security metric (currently 78%).

Score Ranges:

  • 🟢 90-100: Excellent protection
  • 🟡 70-89: Good protection
  • 🔴 Below 70: Review settings needed

Score Factors: Permissions, service status, data security, compliance

🛡️

2. Safety & Auditability Metrics

Two Key Indicators:

  • Safety (92%): Encryption enabled, access controls, compliance active
  • Auditability (85%): Full audit logs, 90-day retention, real-time monitoring

These scores show your current security and compliance posture in real-time.

🔐

3. Permission Management

Control What Data Services Can Access:

  • Profile Data - Personal information
  • Analytics - Behavioral tracking
  • Location - Geographic access
  • Notifications - Alert permissions

Note: Each toggle updates your Trust Score automatically.

🔗

4. Connected Services

Service Status Indicators:

  • 🟢 Active: Connected & operational
  • 🟡 Pending: Awaiting setup/authorization
  • ⚫ Inactive: Disconnected from account

Click services to manage connections and integrations.
